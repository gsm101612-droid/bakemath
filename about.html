ABOUT US

Practical tools for everyday cooking.

BakeMath is a simple collection of kitchen and baking conversion tools designed to make common measurement questions easier to answer.

Our tools provide estimates for everyday use. Ingredient weights can vary with how an ingredient is packed, the measuring method, and the product itself. For best baking consistency, use a kitchen scale and follow the recipe's instructions.

This website is a work in progress. We aim to make the tools clear, useful, and easy to use on both phones and computers.
