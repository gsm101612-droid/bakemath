About BakeUnits

Baking is part science, part creativity—and more fun when the math feels easy.

BakeUnits is a collection of practical kitchen calculators to help home bakers convert measurements, estimate ingredient weights, change oven temperatures, and adjust recipe quantities.

Our approach

We aim to make tools that are quick to understand and useful on a phone or computer. Conversion values are estimates; recipes and ingredient brands may vary.

Accuracy matters

For consistent results, use a kitchen scale when possible and follow recipe-specific guidance.
