About BakeUnits

Welcome to BakeUnits, an independent collection of online tools designed to make kitchen measurements and recipe conversions easier to understand.

Tools for everyday recipes

Recipes often use a mix of cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, grams, milliliters, Fahrenheit, and Celsius. BakeUnits brings common conversions together in one convenient place.

Our tools help you convert cups to approximate gram weights by ingredient, switch between common volume units, convert oven temperatures, scale ingredient quantities, and review common kitchen equivalents.

Accuracy and transparency

Ingredient weights can vary depending on the ingredient and how it is measured. We aim to provide useful estimates and clear explanations, but results are not a substitute for recipe instructions. For consistent baking results, use a kitchen scale when precision matters.
