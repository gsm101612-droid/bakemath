A QUICK NOTE

Measure with confidence

Cooking measurements can vary by ingredient, packing, and measuring technique. For baking, a kitchen scale is often the most consistent way to measure dry ingredients.

Use these converters as helpful estimates, and follow the instructions in your recipe whenever they provide a specific measurement method.
