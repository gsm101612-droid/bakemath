Flour can become compacted in a measuring cup. For consistent baking results, weigh ingredients with a kitchen scale when possible.

Why ingredient choice matters

Cups measure volume, while grams measure weight. Ingredients have different densities, so a cup of flour is much lighter than a cup of granulated sugar. Packing, humidity, grind size, and measuring technique can also affect results.

How to use this converter

  1. Choose the ingredient that matches your recipe.
  2. Enter the amount in US cups or select a quick amount.
